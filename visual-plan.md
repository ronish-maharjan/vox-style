# Visual Plan: History of Nepal Documentary

## 1. Narrative Analysis

**Central Subject:** The political evolution of Nepal from ancient kingdoms to a modern federal democratic republic.

**Central Argument:** Nepal's history is a continuous struggle for sovereignty, unity, and democratic representation — from the unification of fragmented kingdoms through autocratic rule to hard-won democracy.

**Key Historical Beats:**
1. **Ancient Foundations** — Licchavi and Malla periods; cultural and architectural legacy
2. **Unification (1768)** — Prithvi Narayan Shah unifies rival kingdoms into one nation
3. **Rana Oligarchy (1846–1951)** — Hereditary prime ministers reduce monarchy to figureheads
4. **Revolution of 1951** — King Tribhuvan and Nepali Congress overthrow Rana rule
5. **Panchayat Era (1960–1990)** — King Mahendra bans parties, establishes party-less system
6. **Jana Andolan I (1990)** — Mass movement restores multi-party democracy
7. **Maoist Insurgency (1996–2006)** — Decade-long civil war costs 17,000+ lives
8. **Jana Andolan II (2006)** — 19-day uprising forces King Gyanendra to restore parliament
9. **Republic & Constitution (2008–2015)** — Monarchy abolished; federal constitution promulgated

**Emotional Progression:** Fragmentation → Unity → Oppression → Hope → Disillusionment → Conflict → Triumph → Ongoing challenge

**Core Message:** Nepal's sovereignty and democracy were not given — they were fought for, generation after generation.

## 5. Shot Composition Details

### Depth & Layering Strategy
- Backgrounds (`paperBg`, `photoBg`): depth 0.0–0.1
- Main photos (`paperPhoto`, `baseMap`): depth 0.4–0.5
- Documents (`newspaperClip`, `letterPage`): depth 0.5–0.6
- Annotations (`callout`, `markerCircle`, `highlighter`, `stamp`): depth 0.7–0.8, same depth as target
- UI (`lowerThird`, `bigNumber`, `titleCard`): screen space or depth 0.6+

### Camera Movement Rules
- Push-in (1.05–1.15): Default for most shots, reveals detail
- Pull-out (1.08): Final shot only, shows completion
- Pan (0.04–0.10): Across protest photos, maps
- Drift (0.06): Subtle parallax on layered shots
- Hold: On stamps, key numbers — let information land

### Transitions
- `paperSlide` (0.7s): Major era changes (Shots 2→3, 5→6, 7→8)
- `wipe` (0.5s): Within-era shots (Shots 1→2, 3→4, 4→5, 6→7, 8→9)
- No transition: Continuous visual sequences within a shot

### Sound Effects
- Automatic: paper (photos/maps), thud (stamps), pop (numbers), slide (lowerThird), whoosh (wipes), slide (paperSlide), swipe (highlighter), scribble (markerCircle/crossOut)
- Manual emphasis: Crowd ambience on protests, somber drone on civil war, triumphant swell on victories
